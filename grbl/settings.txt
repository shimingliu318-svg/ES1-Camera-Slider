# GRBL 1.1 settings for the DIY Camera Slider (two axes: X = slide, Z = pan)
# Board: Arduino Uno + CNC Shield V3, A4988 drivers at 1/16 microstep. Sender: Universal Gcode Sender (UGS), firmware type GRBL.
# X = slide in mm. Z = pan in DEGREES (every "mm" on Z means degrees).
# Only X and Z are used. The third driver socket stays empty; its settings stay at GRBL defaults.
# Limit switches: 2 mechanical switches, normally open, one on the X limit port and one on the Z limit port (S + GND).
# Homing: config.h homing cycle set to Z then X:
#   #define HOMING_CYCLE_0 (1<<Z_AXIS)
#   #define HOMING_CYCLE_1 (1<<X_AXIS)
# After $H: X = 0 at the LEFT end; moving right gives NEGATIVE X (right end about -1030 on the original build).
# $3, $4 and $6 were not recorded for the original build: set them to suit your wiring and switches.

# --- Steps per mm (Z = steps per degree) ---
# X: 200 x 16 / (20T x 2 mm) = 80
# Z: 200 x 16 x 5 / 360 = 44.444 (16T:80T, 1:5). With a 20T drive pulley (1:4) use 35.556.
$100=80
$102=44.444

# --- Max rate (mm/min; Z = deg/min) ---
$110=12000
$112=1800

# --- Acceleration (mm/s^2; Z = deg/s^2) ---
$120=400
$122=100

# --- Max travel ---
$130=1050           # X max travel on the original build; measure your own rail
$132=260            # Z soft limit span (+/-130 deg)

# --- Limits & homing ---
$20=1               # soft limits on
$21=1               # hard limits on
$22=1               # homing on
$23=1               # X homes at the LEFT end
$24=200
$25=3000
$26=25
$27=10              # pull-off

# $3: not recorded for the original build; set it to suit your wiring and switches
# $4: not recorded for the original build; set it to suit your wiring and switches
$5=0                # limit switches normally open
# $6: not recorded for the original build; set it to suit your wiring and switches
